COURSE 04

Hardware & Robotics

Explore robotics components, Arduino basics, motor control, sensors and the integration of a working robot.

COURSE CONTENT

What you will learn

  • Robotics theory and Arduino basics
  • Motor driver and DC motors
  • Sensors and wiring
  • Programming all four operating modes
  • Circuit diagrams and source code
  • Robot integration, testing and final demonstration
STUDENT BENEFITS

How this course can benefit you

Gain practical exposure to robotics components and wiring, understand how motors and sensors work together, and practise integrating, testing and demonstrating a robot.
